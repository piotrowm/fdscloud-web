The engineering question

The goal is not simply to select the finest mesh that available hardware can run. Mesh resolution should be justified by the phenomena being resolved, verification and sensitivity requirements, relevant FDS guidance and the purpose of the engineering analysis.

What FDSCloud will test

Our real study will use controlled FDS cases and report cell count, D*/dx where applicable, mesh decomposition, CPU configuration, wall-clock time and selected output sensitivity. The objective will be to show the relationship between resolution and compute demand without presenting runtime as a substitute for model validation.
